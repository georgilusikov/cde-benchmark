# v2 preregistration

Primary comparison is B1 vs B2 on 5 domains, 25 artifacts (10 controls + 15 mutations), 3 independent runs, randomized order. Evaluators do not see gold. A separate judge scores DEFECT STATUS (PASS/FAIL), DECISION READINESS (READY/NEEDS_EVIDENCE/BLOCKED), false defect rate, readiness calibration, defect recall, and cost. Stable means correct in >=2/3 runs. Missing evidence is not a defect. Do not use the v1 ACCEPT/REJECT false-rejection metric.

B2 is B1 plus one relational-failure pass and explicit separation of demonstrated defects from evidence gaps. No CDE stages or additional modules are tested.
