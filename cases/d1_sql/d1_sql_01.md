# d1_sql_01\n\nMode: AUDIT\n\n## Brief\nTask: return customers whose sum of paid orders in 2024 is at least 1000. Customers without orders are excluded. Refund is a separate negative amount and reduces the total.\n\n## Artifact\nSELECT c.id, SUM(o.amount) AS total FROM customers c JOIN orders o ON o.customer_id=c.id WHERE o.status='paid' AND o.created_at >= '2024-01-01' AND o.created_at < '2025-01-01' GROUP BY c.id HAVING SUM(o.amount)>=1000;\n
