# d1_sql_03\n\nMode: AUDIT\n\n## Brief\nTask: return customers whose sum of paid orders in 2024 is at least 1000. Customers without orders are excluded. Refund is a separate negative amount and reduces the total.\n\n## Artifact\nSELECT c.id,SUM(o.amount) FROM customers c JOIN orders o ON o.customer_id=c.id JOIN payments p ON p.order_id=o.id WHERE o.created_at >= '2024-01-01' AND o.created_at < '2025-01-01' GROUP BY c.id HAVING SUM(o.amount)>=1000;\n
